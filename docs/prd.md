# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Title

**Smart College Canteen Queue & Ordering System**

---

## Problem Statement

During college breaks, the canteen experiences heavy crowding, resulting in long waiting times, inefficient order management, and uncertainty about food availability. Students often spend a significant portion of their break standing in queues instead of utilizing their time effectively.

The objective of this project is to build a web-based canteen management system that enables students to pre-order food, make online payments, monitor live queue and crowd status, and receive real-time updates about their orders while helping canteen staff manage orders more efficiently.

---

## Target Users

- Students
- Canteen Staff
- Admin

---

## Objectives

- Reduce physical queues during peak hours.
- Enable students to pre-order food.
- Provide real-time order tracking and estimated waiting times.
- Display live crowd status and seat availability.
- Improve canteen operations through efficient order and menu management.

---

## Core Features

### Student

- View the menu
- Search and browse food items
- Place food orders
- Make online payments
- Track order status
- View estimated waiting time before placing an order
- View order history
- Check canteen crowd status and seat availability
- Receive announcements and order notifications

### Admin

#### Menu Management

- Add, edit, or remove menu items
- Update prices
- Enable/disable menu items
- Manage food categories

#### Order Management

- View and manage all orders
- View order history
- Cancel orders (if required)

#### User Management

- View registered users
- Manage staff accounts

#### Analytics

- View total orders
- View total revenue
- View peak ordering hours
- View most ordered items
- View average preparation time

#### Canteen Management

- Update seat availability
- Set canteen status (Open/Closed)
- Set estimated preparation time
- Broadcast announcements to students

---

## Unique Value Proposition

Unlike traditional food ordering systems, this application focuses on reducing canteen congestion through live queue visibility, estimated waiting times, and seat availability. Students can make informed decisions about when to order and visit the canteen, improving both user experience and canteen operations.

---

## Success Criteria

- Reduce waiting time during peak hours.
- Enable students to order and pay online.
- Provide accurate real-time order tracking.
- Improve canteen order management.
- Deliver a responsive and user-friendly experience across desktop and mobile devices.
